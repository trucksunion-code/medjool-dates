The Four Grades

The grade is determined by the percentage of skin separation from the flesh — the key quality and price factor.

PR

Premium

Top commercial grade — plump fruit with firm, intact skin

0 – 10 %
DL

Delight

High quality with slightly higher skin separation

10 – 20 %
CL

Classic

Good quality for daily consumption and trade

20 – 30 %
ST

Standard

Economy grade — higher skin separation

> 30 %
